## Methods

- **Closing-line value (CLV)** is the de-vigged closing probability of the side we took minus the break-even probability of the price we took. Positive means the market moved our way after we flagged the price. Over a season CLV separates skill from luck far sooner than win-loss does, so it is the headline number here.
- **The close** is the other sportsbooks' de-vigged prices in the last snapshot within two hours of kickoff, our own book and the exchanges excluded and Pinnacle weighted four times: for a moneyline their closing moneylines (since 2026-10-29; before that the stored consensus, which included our book), for a spread or total their prices at the exact line we bet (since 2026-10-28; see Rule changes). A bet marked † had fewer than two other books at its line, so its close is our own distribution applied to the consensus closing line, a method that favours us by about half a point.
- **ROI** is per $1 of stake. *Flat* stakes $1 on every bet; *suggested* uses the stake share printed in the report (fractional Kelly on a notional bankroll). The 95% interval is mean ± 1.96 standard errors; when it includes zero, the sample cannot yet tell profit from noise, and we say so.
- **What counts:** every row the daily report recommended at a book we bet at, at the price printed, graded on the final score. Pushes return the stake. Prices the program passed on, promo bets and the author's own bets are not in this table (they are graded separately in the private repo).
- **Market status:** *proven* needs 100 flagged game-sides with CLV two standard errors above zero; *paused* is 10 with CLV two standard errors below zero; everything else is *unproven*. A paused market is not recommended until its model changes.
- **Tamper evidence:** each morning the SHA-256 of every report file is committed to the public mirror before any game starts; the report files follow 7 days later and can be checked against the morning hash.

- **2026-10-03** First recommendations logged (the `bets` table). Stakes are fractional-Kelly shares of a notional bankroll.
- **2026-10-14** Analyst review: a bet needs at least +2c EV per $1 and no favourite shorter than -300; repeated sightings of one price count once; NHL totals include the overtime goal.
- **2026-10-18** Daily SHA-256 manifests of every report begin in the public mirror (reports released after 7 days).
- **2026-10-19** Soccer handicaps and totals Paused after losing to the close; Dixon-Coles replacement pending.
- **2026-10-26** An exchange side with an empty ask book gets no assumed price (two phantom Kalshi dry orders were found and voided). This record page built; the mirror's manifests become append-only.
- **2026-10-28** Closing benchmark for spreads and totals changed, and every past bet restated. Until now the "close" for a spread or total was our own model's distribution applied to the consensus closing line; a study found it leaned toward our side by about half a point, so spread and total CLV read positive when it was not: on the 80 bets whose line two other books had posted, +0.44 reported became -1.10 (24% beat the close); over every bet with a close, golf included, the headline went from +0.47 to +0.06. The close is now the other books' de-vigged prices at the bet's own line in the last pre-kickoff snapshot (our book excluded, Pinnacle weighted 4x). Where fewer than two other books posted that line the old method remains and the row is marked †. Moneyline CLV was never affected. The haircut that shrinks our edges is now fitted against the close only (never on 0/1 outcomes) and moves faster (prior weight 300 -> 100 games).
- **2026-10-29** Pricing and grading changed after a study of 99,000 soft-book prices against the close, and every past bet restated. (1) Exchange prices (Kalshi, Polymarket) leave the consensus a book is judged against: they were inside it, contrary to the published method. (2) A spread or total is now priced against the other books' prices at the same line when at least three post it, instead of our own distribution's translation of the consensus line; the study found this benchmark's EV predicts closing-line value (55-76% of its 2c+ prices beat the close) where the translated one did not. (3) The moneyline close is the other sportsbooks' de-vigged closing prices, our own book and the exchanges excluded, Pinnacle 4x (the stored consensus had included both). (4) Soccer's three-way consensus is weighted like every other market (Pinnacle 4x; it was an unweighted mean). (5) Feed hygiene: totals outside a sport's range, spread prices without a line and two-way prices summing past 112% are discarded before pricing.
