Methods

  • Closing-line value (CLV) is the de-vigged closing probability of the side we took minus the break-even probability of the price we took. Positive means the market moved our way after we flagged the price. Over a season CLV separates skill from luck far sooner than win-loss does, so it is the headline number here.
  • ROI is per $1 of stake. Flat stakes $1 on every bet; suggested uses the stake share printed in the report (fractional Kelly on a notional bankroll). The 95% interval is mean ± 1.96 standard errors; when it includes zero, the sample cannot yet tell profit from noise, and we say so.
  • What counts: every row the daily report recommended at a book we bet at, at the price printed, graded on the final score. Pushes return the stake. Prices the program passed on, promo bets and the author's own bets are not in this table (they are graded separately in the private repo).
  • Market status: proven needs 100 flagged game-sides with CLV two standard errors above zero; paused is 10 with CLV two standard errors below zero; everything else is unproven. A paused market is not recommended until its model changes.
  • Tamper evidence: each morning the SHA-256 of every report file is committed to the public mirror before any game starts; the report files follow 7 days later and can be checked against the morning hash.
